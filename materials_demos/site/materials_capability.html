What we don't do

  • A molecular cluster is not the periodic material; a classical potential is not DFT; a geometric predictor is not docking. Every proxy is labeled as one.
  • Template defaults — cutoffs, k-meshes, timesteps, functionals — are reasonable starting points, not converged production settings. We flag what still needs convergence on your system.
  • We report what validated and what didn't. A packaging demo built on cheap forces proves the plumbing; the physics still needs the real AIMD.
