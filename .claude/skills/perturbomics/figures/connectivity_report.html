One axis for drug & CRISPR perturbations — and the combinations that reverse a disease

Drug perturbation (LINCS L1000), CRISPR knockout, and a disease state are reduced to one object — a signed ranked gene signature — so a single connectivity score ranks what reverses the disease, and combination analysis finds drug + gene pairs that reverse more together than alone.

The reduction

Every modality becomes the same signature, then one score compares any two

This is the whole idea: connectivity never looks at how a perturbation was made, only at the ranked signature it produced. So a drug profile, a CRISPR knockout and a disease state are all just ranked gene lists — and the Connectivity-Map weighted score (WTCS) compares any pair.

Reading the top hit
Canertinib (a pan-ErbB/EGFR inhibitor) is the strongest reversing drug that also dominates the combinations; trichostatin A, the #2 reverser, is an HDAC inhibitor — an actively studied antifibrotic class. The best genetic partner, CDK13 knockout, adds +3.3% coverage beyond canertinib alone: the drug and the knockout correct partly different genes. That complementarity — not the raw coverage — is what makes it a combination lead.

Case C · integration

03From signature to lead — four axes, one ranking

Connectivity says what to perturb; on its own a top hit is one line of evidence. Fusing three more — pathway control, molecular engageability, clinical status — turns the ranked signature into a prioritised, multi-scale lead table. Each axis is an independent experiment; a candidate that clears all four is far more credible than a connectivity outlier.

Why the ranking flips — the whole point
procaterol tops the transcriptomic axis alone (−1.000) but is an off-pathway β2-agonist → 3rd. trichostatin A and pevonedistat are the most potent (IC50 ~1–5 nM, fetched live) yet their targets aren't network-control nodes. Canertinib rises to #1 with weaker connectivity — the only hit on a control node (EGFR, genuinely implicated in IPF) that is also potent and phase-3. No single axis ranks it first; the fusion does.
Live provenance: engageability & clinical fetched this session — canertinib EGFR IC50 1.5 nM (ChEMBL CHEMBL31965, phase 3, 3 trials); pevonedistat NAE 4.7 nM (41 trials); trichostatin HDAC 1.4 nM. ⚠️ Caveats: the network axis is an illustrative IPF core/switch set, not a computed M1 quotient; the Boltz binding job (async) was not run, so engageability uses ChEMBL potency alone.
Case D · drug repurposing

04Reuse an approved drug for a new indication

The same reversal logic, one filter tighter: keep only drugs that are already launched (Drug Repurposing Hub) and not already used for the disease. A launched drug that reverses the IPF signature from a different indication is a repurposing candidate — scored |WTCS| × phase × indication-novelty.

Two kinds of repurposing hit — the method finds both
dasatinib, nominated from transcriptomics alone, turns out to be genuinely in an IPF trial (NCT02874989, Dasatinib + Quercetin senolytic pilot) — the screen recovered a real repurposing bet, a strong sanity check. neratinib (an EGFR inhibitor, like canertinib in Case C) reverses IPF but has no IPF trials — a novel white-space hypothesis with mechanistic backing.
⚠️ Caveat: corticosteroids (betamethasone, budesonide) also score high — an anti-inflammatory signature match — yet steroids are not effective in IPF specifically. A reminder that signature reversal is a hypothesis generator, not efficacy; every hit needs disease-specific validation.
Honesty ledger

What is computed vs. what is hypothesis

The discipline this pipeline enforces on every claim — deterministic computation is never blurred with biological interpretation.

✓ RIGOROUS Deterministic

  • Every WTCS and coverage number — exact statistics on the supplied vectors.
  • The consensus signatures — reproducible aggregation of real GEO/L1000 data.
  • The full run reproduces bit-for-bit with a fixed hash seed.

⚠ HYPOTHESIS Needs validation

  • “Reverses the disease signature ⇒ therapeutic” — a transcriptional similarity, not an efficacy claim.
  • “This drug + knockout pair is synergistic” — requires a real combination assay (Bliss/Loewe on a dose matrix).
  • Coarse ±1 gene-list signatures and a heterogeneous reference need a null model / normalization before any hit is believed.
