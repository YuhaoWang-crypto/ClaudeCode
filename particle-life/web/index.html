Reading the map

  • Blobs / cells — reciprocal self-attraction; frozen once formed.
  • Chains, snakes — cyclic bonding graph i↔i±1; thickness = α.
  • Chasers — Aab>0>Aba; net internal force ≠ 0.
  • Rotors — cyclic pursuit (rock–paper–scissors); net internal torque ≠ 0.
  • Gas — no unstable k; Re λ(k) < 0 everywhere.
